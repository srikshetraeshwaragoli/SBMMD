History & Sacred Heritage

Shri Kshetra Eshwaragoli has stood as a beacon of faith and spiritual serenity for centuries. Nestled amidst peaceful surroundings, the temple attracts thousands of devotees seeking divine grace, inner peace, and spiritual uplifting.
